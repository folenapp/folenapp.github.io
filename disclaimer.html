Medical Disclaimer

Effective date: October 6, 2026

FoliQ is a tracking and habit tool, not a doctor. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it is not a medical device.

Scores are estimates

The Hair Score, sub-scores (density, hairline, crown, thickness), risk estimate, momentum and forecast are calculated from your own answers and the habits you log. They are not the result of a clinical examination, and your photos are not analysed to produce a diagnosis. Two people with the same answers will get the same estimate even if their scalps are different.

Talk to a professional

Hair loss can have many causes, including genetics, hormones, thyroid problems, nutritional deficiencies, medication, stress and scalp conditions. If you notice sudden, patchy or rapid hair loss, pain, itching, redness or scaling, or if you are worried, please see a doctor or board-certified dermatologist.

Treatments

FoliQ may remind you about treatments you decide to use. It does not prescribe or recommend medication. Treatments such as minoxidil and finasteride can have side effects and are not suitable for everyone. Only start, change or stop a treatment after talking to a qualified healthcare professional, and follow their instructions and the product label.

Statistics

General statistics shown in the app (for example, how common hair loss is at a certain age) come from published research and are population averages. They describe groups of people, not you individually.

Emergencies

FoliQ is not for emergencies. If you have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.
