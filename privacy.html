Folen

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

The short version. We collect only what the app needs to track your hair over time. Your front photo is cropped on your phone so your face is never stored or uploaded. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Folen ("we", "us") is an app operated by Mateo Iglesias, an independent developer based in Spain, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: foliqapp@gmail.com.

Some of this information (your hair condition and related answers) may be considered health-related data. We process it only because you choose to enter it and give your explicit consent when you use the app for this purpose (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising and there are no ads in Folen.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use your photos to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not diagnose medical conditions (see our Medical Disclaimer).

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers in the United States. When data is transferred from the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) offered by our providers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, answers, activity, score history and photos are permanently deleted from our database and storage immediately. Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days. Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 12 months and are not linked to your name or email.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Access, correct or export your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Photo analysis: it only runs if you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time in Profile → Settings → Photo analysis.
  • EU/UK residents have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).
  • California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Photos are kept in a private storage bucket that only your account can access. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Age requirement

Folen is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will notify you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
